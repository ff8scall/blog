## Strategic Deep-Dive

idia’s unprecedented ascent to market dominance is fueled by a product roadmap that moves at a pace previously thought impossible in the hardware world. By compressing its architectural iteration cycle to a single year, Nvidia is effectively forcing its entire supply chain to operate in a permanent state of emergency. While this strategy secures Nvidia’s competitive moat against rivals like AMD and Intel, it is creating a dangerous level of systemic strain for its manufacturing and component partners.
